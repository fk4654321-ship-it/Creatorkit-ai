Privacy

Everything is processed locally in your browser. CreatorKit AI does not send what you type to any server, and it has no accounts, tracking or cookies.

FAQ

Is this a real AI model?

No. CreatorKit AI uses fixed templates and simple rules written into the page. The text changes with your inputs, but nothing is generated by a live AI model.

Will these titles or hooks make my videos go viral?

No tool can promise that. Treat the results as drafts to adapt to your content and audience.

Do I need to be online or sign in?

No sign-in is needed. Once the page has loaded, the tools work without any network requests.

Why are there [brackets] in my script?

The tool does not know your facts, so it leaves marked spots for your own points. This avoids inventing information for you.

Why do I get the same results every time?

Results are deterministic. The same topic and settings always give the same output. Change a word or a setting to get a different mix.

About

CreatorKit AI is a free, early MVP built to take the blank-page pressure off short-form creators. It is a single static page with no backend, no paid services and no external code. The name refers to the creator-toolkit idea; the tools themselves are simple, transparent templates.
